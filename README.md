Here's a ready-to-paste Lovable prompt:

Build a professional, modern marketing website for S&S Tech Solutions Hub, a Kenya-based creative and digital solutions agency based in Nairobi (offices in CBD and Westlands).

BRAND OVERVIEW
S&S Tech Solutions Hub is co-owned by Sheila (Chief Technology & Innovation Officer) and Sam (Chief Marketing & Frontend Officer). Tagline: "web · design · development · digital solutions." Closing line to use somewhere near the footer: "Building Brands, One Design at a Time."

Tone: clean, professional, confident — speaks to entrepreneurs, SMEs, campaigns, and corporate clients across Kenya. Modern SaaS/agency aesthetic, not corporate-stiff. Mobile-first responsive design.

PAGES / SECTIONS

1. Hero Section
- Headline focused on helping businesses "get noticed and get results" through design and websites
- Subheadline mentioning the two core pillars: Web Design & Development + Graphic Design & Branding
- Primary CTA: "Get a Free Quote" / "Book a Discovery Call"
- Secondary CTA: WhatsApp contact button

2. Services Overview (6 cards/icons)
- Graphic Design & Branding — logos, brand identity, marketing collateral
- Event & Party Posters — invites, flyers, social graphics
- Political & Campaign Materials — candidate branding, civic awareness
- Festive & Personal Greetings
- Business & Recruitment Ads
- Web Design & Development — websites & digital platforms

3. Website Development Pricing (4-tier pricing table, feature-comparison style)
- STARTER — KES 15,000 / USD 115: up to 3 pages, single template, responsive, contact form, 1 revision, 2 weeks support, 5–7 day delivery
- BASIC — KES 25,000 / USD 190: up to 5 pages, basic SEO, Google Maps, SSL support, Analytics, 2 revisions, 1 month support, 7–10 day delivery
- PREMIUM — KES 65,000 / USD 490: up to 10 semi-custom pages, CMS + training, advanced SEO, blog, newsletter integration, 4 revisions, 3 months support, 14–21 day delivery — mark as "Most Popular"
- PRO — KES 140,000 / USD 1,050: up to 20 fully custom pages, full brand kit, e-commerce (50 products), booking system, CRM integration, unlimited revisions, 6 months priority support, dedicated account manager, 21–30 day delivery
Include a toggle or note for KES/USD, and a footnote that add-ons (extra pages, maintenance, e-commerce setup) are quoted separately.

4. Graphic Design Pricing (simple table)
- Church Poster: KES 800–2,000
- Event Poster: KES 1,000–2,500
- Club Poster: KES 1,500–3,000
- Business Promotion Poster: KES 2,000–4,000
- Product Advertisement Poster: KES 2,500–5,000
- Logo Design: from KES 1,500

5. Portfolio / Design Showcase
Masonry or grid gallery with category filters: Event & Occasion Posters, Political & Campaign Branding, Festive & Personal Greetings, Business & Organisational Ads. Use placeholder image blocks labeled with the project names (e.g. "Creative Vibes — Day Event Poster," "Team Victor Campaign," "Pizza Time Product Poster") until real images are uploaded.

6. Our Process (6-step horizontal or vertical timeline)
Discovery Call (free) → Proposal & Agreement → Deposit → Design & Build → Review & Revisions → Launch & Handover

7. About / Founders Section
Two-person founder spotlight: Sheila (CTIO — backend, infrastructure, innovation) and Sam (CMFO — frontend, marketing, client relations). Emphasize the blend of strategic thinking, clean design, and technology.

8. Contact Section
- Phone/WhatsApp: +254 713 268806 and +254 115 323 604 (clickable WhatsApp links)
- Email: sstechsolutionsa@gmail.com
- Location: Nairobi, Kenya (CBD & Westlands)
- Contact form: name, email, service interested in (dropdown), message
- Embed a "Book a Free Discovery Call" CTA

9. Footer
Logo/name, quick links, social icons, contact info, tagline "Building Brands, One Design at a Time."

DESIGN GUIDELINES
- Color palette: professional, tech-forward (e.g. deep navy/charcoal + one vibrant accent color — teal, orange, or electric blue) — you choose something distinctive, not generic blue-and-white SaaS default
- Typography: modern sans-serif, strong heading hierarchy
- Use smooth scroll animations and hover states on service/portfolio cards
- Sticky navbar with anchor links to Services, Pricing, Portfolio, Process, About, Contact
- Fully responsive for mobile, since much of the client base browses via phone/WhatsApp
- Fast-loading, since this represents a company that sells websites — it needs to look genuinely premium

FUNCTIONALITY
- Working contact form (or mailto/WhatsApp fallback)
- Pricing tables with tier comparison, "Most Popular" highlight on Premium
- Smooth anchor navigation
- WhatsApp click-to-chat buttons throughout
